### 📦 Mission 02. Project PARAKH — *Counterfeit Detection & Legal Metrology Act Compliance Platform*

<p align="left">
  <a href="https://github.com/jitendrachoudhary1401-hue/Parakh.git">
    <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/🔗_GITHUB_REPO-Project_PARAKH-05050A?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=E23636&labelColor=05050A&color=E23636" alt="Project PARAKH Repo"/>

> **🕷️ Mission Briefing:** Equipping Spider-Suit optical lenses with deep transformer models! **Project PARAKH** is an automated anti-counterfeiting and statutory compliance inspection platform that validates packaged commodities against **Legal Metrology Act** regulations using multimodal Computer Vision, OCR, and Named Entity Recognition.

| Suit Module | Engineering Implementation & Real-World Heroic Impact |
| :--- | :--- |
| **🔬 Micro-Anomaly Packaging Detection (`ViT`)** | Integrates `google/vit-base-patch16-224` Vision Transformers to inspect product packaging textures, logos, and print tolerances to flag counterfeit anomalies. |
| **🤗 Transformer Entity Extraction (`BERT-NER`)** | Employs `dslim/bert-base-NER` alongside multi-angle OCR pipelines to parse noisy packaging text into structured fields (MRP, Net Quantity, Mfg Date, Origin, Consumer Care). |
| **⚖️ Statutory Legal Metrology Rule Engine** | Automatically cross-verifies extracted declarations against statutory packaged commodity mandates and generates instant compliance audit reports. |
